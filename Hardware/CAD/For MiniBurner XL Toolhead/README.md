# This are the parts needet to use my toolhead, MiniBurner XL, with Klicky-Changer.

You can find Miniburner XL Toolhead [HERE](https://github.com/printicus/For-Voron-Printers/tree/main/MiniBurner%20XL%20Toolhead).

## BOM for Dock:
- Filament ABS or ASA
- M3 Heat Inserts x2(voron specs)
- 6x3x2 Magnets x4 - N-S oriented all in the same direction. The pairs for this magnets will be on the toolhead
- M2x10 x4 Countersunk Selftapping Screws for magnets
- M3 Hammerhead T-nuts x2
- M3x8 x4
- Silicon for the silicon pad
- 50x10x0.4 spring steel for the silicon pad.

## BOM for Backplate
- Filament ABS or ASA
- M3 Heat Inserts x2(voron specs)
- M3x4x12 Pins x3 - https://vi.aliexpress.com/item/1005007596796680.html
- M3x10 x2 screws - for the upper pins(see CAD)
- M3x8 x1 screw - for the lower pin
- Pogopins - The pair that comes with the one mounted on the shuttle. On the Backplate will be mounted the one with the springy pins. The pogopins have magnets on both ends, and can be paired only in one direction.
- M1.5x4 x2 Self Tapping Screws for pogopins(the same used on the shuttle) - https://vi.aliexpress.com/item/1005003934120055.html
- 6x3x2 Magnets - two with N-S oriented in one direction and two in the other direction(see picture in Images folder)
                https://www.buyneomagnets.com/p/6mm-dia-x-3mm-thick-with-countersunk-hole-2mm-n35-strong-countersunk-neodymium-disc-magnets-countersunk-ring-rare-earth-magnet
- M2x8 x4 Countersunk Self Tapping Screws for magnets - https://vi.aliexpress.com/item/1005006676743568.html

## Printing:

4 walls, 5 Top layers, 5 Bottom layers, 40-50% infill.

Shuttle and Backplate should be printed in the same XY orientation. No mather how good your printer is, there would allways be a size diference between the same dimension on X and Y.

## For the Shuttle and Backplate - The design gave you the option for 4 pairs of magnets, but you should only use them all if your magnets are weak. No need to use 4 pairs of magnets. For normal use two magnets on the shuttle, one on each side, and two on the backplate are enough.

## Using the silicon mold for Miniburner XL toolchanger dock.

[![Silicon Mold](https://img.youtube.com/vi/6CMpIc2Bso8/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CMpIc2Bso8)

Silicon I used: - https://www.amazon.de/dp/B01MZ7EO1V?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1


Its a very high temp resisting silicon. No release agent needed. It does not stick to annything, and can easy be removed. One or two holes drilled in the springsteel strip before molding it in the pad for the dock, will help.
